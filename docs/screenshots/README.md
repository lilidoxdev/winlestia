# Screenshots

Real captures of Winlestia 0.9.0 on a Windows 11 session (3440x1440). Nothing here is a mock-up.

Full-desktop captures are scaled to 2560 pixels wide; `tools.png` and `panels.png` are at native size. The native captures are in `Winlestia-0.9.0-screenshots.zip` on the [v0.9.0 release](https://github.com/lilidoxdev/winlestia/releases/tag/v0.9.0).

`video-16x9.jpg` and `video-9x16.jpg` are frames taken from the demo videos.
